Fictional retail dataset Jan–Apr 2026 Excel Financial analysis Business reporting

Overview

An analysis of the financial performance of a fictional retail business, Nova Retail Solution, using a made-up dataset covering January to April 2026. The goal was to study revenue, costs and profitability month by month and summarise the findings in a clear business report.

Approach

  • Organised monthly sales, cost and expense data in Excel
  • Calculated gross profit, operating profit and margins
  • Compared month-over-month trends
  • Summarised the findings in a business report
